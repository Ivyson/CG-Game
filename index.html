PAC-MAN

3D WebGL Edition

Navigate the maze. Escape the ghosts. Dominate the classic.

Navigation System

ARROWS Navigate the maze
ENTER Pause or resume game
MOUSE Rotate camera view
SCROLL Zoom in and out

Game Objectives

  • Consume all pellets to achieve victory
  • Collect power-ups for 12-second invulnerability
  • Evade four intelligent ghosts
  • Survive with only one life
